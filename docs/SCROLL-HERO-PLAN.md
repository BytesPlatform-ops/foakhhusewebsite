# Scroll Hero — Corrected Plan (v4): Abstract-Real Premium

_Replaces v3. Short, punchy, luxury-campaign aesthetic. 2026-07-28._
_Reference captures this cycle: hashgraphvc.com (thin luminous line
geometry on deep ground, extreme restraint), hubtown, monolog._

## 1. Corrected visual direction

REAL BUILDING IN FRONT · ABSTRACT CINEMATIC ATMOSPHERE BEHIND.
The silhouette stays architectural terracotta — the most "real" object in
frame. Everything energetic becomes light: bloom, haze, luminous streaks,
soft arcs. Nothing behind the building has a hard edge.

## 2. Revised art-direction mood

Luxury architectural campaign image. Hubtown's aspiration + MONOLOG's
editorial type + 21hrs' masking depth + the films' motion cues +
Spider-Verse read ONLY as glow / chromatic edge / colour vibration.
Poster, not explainer. Cinematic, not comic.

## 3. Updated hero composition

180–200svh desktop (sticky 100svh), ~150svh mobile, 4 beats, ~5s feel.
Building centred at ~38–42% viewport width. Oversized words pass behind
the blocks; the final headline, copy, CTAs settle lower-left; facts rail
bottom. No giant road, no empty columns, no text across windows.

## 4. Background treatment

All flat planes are REMOVED. Replacement layers (back→front):
deep cobalt atmospheric glow (large blurred radial, upper field) · warm
solar bloom (radial, behind rooftop, drifts to the solar side in beat 3) ·
coral light haze (low, soft) · one translucent diagonal gradient plane ·
two abstract light arcs (thin gradient strokes, blurred, barely moving) ·
cyan airflow streaks (2–3 luminous curves drawing in beat 3) · a single
tiny orchid glint · depth fog rising from the base · static grain.
Halftone: deleted. Every colour exists as light, never as a sticker.

## 5. Typography behaviour

Beat 1: eyebrow + A NEW RHYTHM at 12% opacity behind the building.
Beat 2: WHERE (quiet, 15%) above NATURE (active, ~90%) — both BEHIND the
silhouette so the blocks genuinely mask letters. Beat 3: WIND MEETS
SUNLIGHT, words activating sequentially (MONOLOG hierarchy), inactive at
~18%. Beat 4: WHERE NATURE / POWERS / MODERN LIVING. — editorial scale,
lower-left, in front, never pasted across the façade. Few words total.

## 6. Four-state scroll structure

| Beat | Range | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Quiet arrival | 0–20% | Cream ground, outline draws, soft shadow, scale 0.94→1, eyebrow |
| 2 Atmosphere awakens | 20–45% | Cobalt glow first, solar bloom follows, coral haze, terracotta fill + warm edge light, subtle chromatic edge on entry |
| 3 Energy hint | 45–72% | Refined turbine silhouettes (slight rotor turn), minimal ink kite on an elegant cable, one cyan streak to the gap, one rooftop light sweep, tiny green node |
| 4 Final frame | 72–100% | Everything settles: windows warm, glow steadies, headline + copy + CTAs + facts; still frame holds, then unpins |

## 7. Abstract-but-real, concretely

Building: keep geometry, deepen the facade gradient, single soft
edge-glow (blurred stroke, colour drifting cyan→gold→coral at very low
opacity), base contact shadow, ≤1.5° rotation, rear block 6–10px slower.
Atmosphere: only radial/linear gradients at 30–60% opacity with large
blur radii; arcs and streaks are 1.5–3px luminous strokes with soft
glow, not shapes. Motion: stagger + settle; nothing loops in beat 4.

## 8. Removed from the cartoon version

Flat cobalt rounded rectangle, flat yellow disc, flat coral slab, flat
green blob, aqua pill, halftone dot field, coral speed-line marks, the
red/white striped cartoon kite (replaced by a minimal ink silhouette),
CAPTURE/CHANNEL/CIRCULATE/COOL pills (belongs to the tunnel chapter
below), the 5-word giant stack (replaced by per-beat words), the internal
corridor x-ray state, and ~320svh of scroll length.
